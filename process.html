Our Process

How we go from idea to launch.

A structured, transparent process so you always know what's happening next and when your site goes live.

1

Requirement Discussion

We start with a call to understand your business, your customers and what the website needs to achieve — more leads, more bookings, more sales.

2

Planning

We map the sitemap, content requirements and technical scope, and share a clear timeline before any design work begins.

3

UI Design

Custom visual design built around your brand — colours, typography and layout chosen for your specific audience, not a generic template.

4

Development

Our developers build the site with clean, modern code, optimised for speed, security and search engines from the start.

5

Testing

Every page is tested across browsers and devices — phone, tablet, laptop and desktop — before anything goes live.

6

Deployment

We handle domain connection, hosting setup and the actual go-live, so launch day is stress-free for you.

7

Support

After launch, we stay available for fixes, updates and questions — with maintenance plans for ongoing peace of mind.
