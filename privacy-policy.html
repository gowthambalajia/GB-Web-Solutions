Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

1. Introduction

GB Web Solutions ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and how we use it.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit through our forms, such as name, email and phone number.
  • Project details you share when requesting a quote.
  • Basic usage data such as pages visited and browser type, collected via standard analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, deliver services and, where you've opted in, send occasional updates about our work.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to remember preferences such as theme selection and to understand aggregate site usage. You can decline non-essential cookies via the cookie banner.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with trusted service providers (such as hosting or payment processors) strictly to deliver the services you've requested.

6. Data Retention

We retain enquiry and project information for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations, after which it is securely deleted.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing hello@gbwebsolutions.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with a new "last updated" date above.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@gbwebsolutions.com.
